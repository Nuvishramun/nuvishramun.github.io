# Research

## Research interests

### Groups and finite geometry

Group actions on generalised quadrangles, symmetry in incidence structures, and classification problems.

### Cayley graphs and symmetry

Cayley graphs, semiregular automorphisms, and the connections between finite groups and graph structure.

### Combinatorial perspectives

Algebraic and combinatorial methods for understanding highly symmetric discrete objects.
